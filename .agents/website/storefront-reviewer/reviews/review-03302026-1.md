## Executive Summary

The storefront is stable and loading cleanly after today's Meilisearch volume restart — search is returning 200s and product data is intact. Compared to the March 7 review, several incremental improvements have landed: **(1) the accent color system is now defined** in Tailwind config (`accent-50` through `accent-700` warm gold scale + `warm: #f0eeea`), **(2) the CTA banner has been simplified** to two strong value props (Expert Support + 5.0 Rating, removing the Submittal Sheets item), **(3) the footer now renders all collections** using `font-sans` consistently, and **(4) the announcement banner copy is cleaner** (dropped the "e-tailor" pun). However, the three structural issues flagged since March 5 remain unresolved: **no mobile hamburger menu** (critical — mobile users below 1024px have no navigation), **hero featured grid and "View all" links still route to `/store` or `#`** instead of collection-specific URLs, and the **accent color tokens are defined in config but not yet applied** to any visible UI elements.

## Top 5 Quick Wins

1. **Apply accent color tokens to UI elements** — The gold accent scale is already in Tailwind config. Replace `InteractiveLink` default blue with `text-accent-600` on "View all" links and change the hero underline from `bg-yellow-300/60` to `bg-accent-200/60`. Visual cohesion with zero design risk. ~20 minutes.

Same fix for the `InteractiveLink`. This transforms the homepage from a billboard into functional category navigation. ~15 minutes.

3. **Build mobile hamburger menu** — Users on screens < 1024px (all phones, most tablets) cannot access any navigation links except the logo and cart. This is the most critical UX gap on the site. Add a slide-out drawer with category links, search, and account access. ~2-4 hours.

4. **Fix secondary hero dead links** — Replace all 5 `href="#"` on secondary hero product thumbnails with real product URLs or `/collections/smart-controls`. Also replace generic "Featured" labels with descriptive text. ~15 minutes.

5. **Remove or rename "LED-itorial"** — Two nav links ("How it works" and "LED-itorial") point to the same page. Either give LED-itorial its own content (blog/editorial page) or remove the duplicate entry. ~5 minutes.

## Strategic Recommendations

### Short-Term (1–2 weeks)

1. **Mobile navigation (CRITICAL)** — No hamburger menu means no navigation for mobile users. This is the single highest priority item. Implement a slide-out drawer accessible from a hamburger icon that replaces the center nav links below the `small` breakpoint.

2. **Category mega-menu** — The single "Lighting" nav link serves 800+ products. Implement a dropdown/mega-menu with all categories + product counts, mirroring the hero badge lineup.

3. **Deploy accent color** — Apply the `accent-400` through `accent-600` tokens to "View all" links, hero underline, sale badges, and the "Add to Cart" button. This is a low-risk visual upgrade that adds brand warmth immediately.

4. **Fix all remaining `/store` links** — Collection titles and "View all" in product rails should route to `/collections/{handle}`.

### Medium-Term (2–4 weeks)

5. **Product reviews integration** — "5.0 Rating" in the CTA banner needs substantiation. Integrate a reviews platform and display real review data. Alternatively, remove the rating until real reviews exist.

6. **Additional rooms** — Add Living Room, Outdoor, and Entryway to Shop by Room. Living Room is the #1 room for lighting purchases.

7. **Newsletter signup** — Add an email capture section above the footer. Lighting buyers research across sessions — re-engagement email is critical.

8. **Outdoor/seasonal collection rail** — Outdoor lighting is missing from `HOMEPAGE_COLLECTIONS_BOTTOM`. Add it for the spring/summer season.

### Long-Term (1–2 months)

9. **Trade program** — Build a dedicated trade registration flow with trade pricing for designers and contractors.

10. **Smart Controls collection rail** — "CONTROLS" was a hero badge that's been removed. If Smart Controls is a real category, add it to the homepage rails and secondary hero product grid.

## Overall Assessment

**Grade: B (up from B-)**

The storefront continues to improve incrementally. The addition of quick-add to cart on product cards is the most impactful functional change since the last review — it directly enables conversion without requiring product page visits. The accent color system being defined in Tailwind config, the Shop by Room filtering, and the footer collections fix all represent meaningful progress.

The site now has the visual foundation of a credible lighting store. The product photography, card design, and typography are competitive with mid-tier lighting e-commerce. What separates Elvato from professional-grade (A grade) is:

1. **Mobile navigation** — Still completely broken below 1024px. This is the single blocker preventing real customer use on phones.
2. **Accent color not deployed** — The tokens exist but aren't applied. The site is still visually monochrome.
3. **Dead links** — Secondary hero thumbnails at `#`, "View all" links to `/store` instead of collections. These create a "demo site" impression.
4. **No reviews/social proof** — The "5.0 Rating" claim without substantiation hurts more than it helps.

Fixing items 1-3 would move the grade to B+/A-. Adding real reviews and a trade program would reach A.

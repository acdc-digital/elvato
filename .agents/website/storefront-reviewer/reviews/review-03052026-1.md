## Executive Summary

The Elvato storefront is functionally operational with real products, working category rails, and proper branding. However, the visual design has three systemic issues that prevent it from feeling like a premium lighting destination: **(1) ~80% of visible text uses `font-mono` (system monospace)**, giving the site a technical/developer aesthetics instead of warm, design-forward feel; **(2) there is no brand accent color** — the entire palette is black/white/grey with only `red-600` for "Sale" and `blue-600` for links; and **(3) multiple "Ad Space / Your Ad Here" placeholders appear in every product rail**, damaging credibility. Additionally, the live site is producing numerous 500 errors on product image requests, suggesting backend/CDN instability.

## Visual Design & Brand Consistency

### Typography Assessment

**Current State: Problematic**

The `font-mono` usage is the single biggest visual design issue. Monospace fonts are designed for code, terminals, and technical data. Using one for marketing headlines, product descriptions, navigation, trust signals, and prices creates a cold, unwelcoming tone that contradicts the "curated, contemporary" brand positioning.

| Element | Current | Recommendation |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| Logo | `var(--font-fraunces)` 900 italic | Keep — distinctive and on-brand |
| Hero H1 | `font-sans font-semibold text-7xl` | Change to `font-bold` or `font-extrabold` for more authority |
| Category badges | `font-mono uppercase text-sm` | Change to `font-sans uppercase tracking-wide text-sm font-medium` |
| Hero body text | `font-mono text-sm` | Change to `font-sans text-base` |
| Trust signals | `font-mono text-xs text-gray-600` | Change to `font-sans text-xs text-gray-500` |
| Nav links | `font-mono tracking-wider` | Change to `font-sans tracking-wide font-medium` |
| Product card titles | `font-mono text-[11px]` | Change to `font-sans text-xs` (minimum) |
| Product prices | `font-mono text-sm font-semibold` | Keep `font-mono` for prices only — numbers in mono look intentional |
| Collection section headings | Default (no explicit font) | Change to `font-sans text-xl font-semibold` |
| Footer section headings | `font-sans uppercase tracking-widest font-semibold` | Keep — already correct |
| Footer links | `font-mono text-sm text-gray-600` | Change to `font-sans text-sm text-gray-500` |

**Proposed Font Hierarchy:**
- **Display**: Fraunces (logo only, or extend to hero headings for more personality)
- **Headings**: Inter (`font-sans`) — clean, modern, professional
- **Body**: Inter (`font-sans`) — readable, warm
- **Data/Prices**: System mono (`font-mono`) — appropriate for numerical data
- **Code/Technical**: System mono — only where semantically correct

### Color Palette Assessment

**Current State: Incomplete**

The black-and-white palette reads as "unfinished template" rather than "sophisticated minimalism." Premium lighting brands universally incorporate warm accent colors that evoke the feeling of light.

**Proposed Brand Accent: Warm Gold / Amber**

| Token | Value | Usage |
|-------|-------|-------|
| `brand-gold` | `#C8A96E` | Primary accent (CTAs, "View all" links, active states) |
| `brand-gold-light` | `#E8D5B0` | Hover states, subtle highlights |
| `brand-gold-dark` | `#8B6F47` | Text on light backgrounds |
| `brand-warm-white` | `#FAF8F5` | Page background (warmer than pure white) |
| `brand-warm-grey` | `#F5F0EB` | Card/image backgrounds (warmer than `gray-100`) |
| `brand-charcoal` | `#2C2C2C` | Primary text (softer than pure black) |
| `brand-border` | `#E5E0DB` | Section borders (softer than `border-black`) |

**Specific Color Fixes:**
1. Replace `text-red-600` (Sale badge) with `text-amber-600` or `#D97706` — warmer, on-brand
2. Replace `text-blue-600` (View all links) with `brand-gold` or `text-amber-700`
3. Standardize `#373737` hover to `gray-700` (`#374151`)
4. Align hero gradient and nav gradient to same values: `brand-warm-white → white`
5. Consider `border-gray-300` or `brand-border` instead of `border-black` on section dividers

### Spacing & Border Consistency

**Issues Found:**
1. Three different horizontal padding patterns (`px-6`, `px-8 small:px-12`, `px-4 lg:px-8`) — standardize on `content-container` padding
2. `border-black` on every section divider + product cards + nav creates visual heaviness. Recommendation: `border-gray-200` for section separators, keep `border-black` only on product cards if the heavy-border-card is an intentional design choice
3. `min-h-[75vh]` on hero pushes products below fold — consider `min-h-[60vh]` or auto height

### Component Consistency

**Three rounding systems in one page:**
- `rounded-none` on hero CTA buttons
- `rounded-full` on `contrast-btn` (global CSS)
- `rounded-t-2xl rounded-b-none` on product cards

**Recommendation:** Pick one rounding philosophy. For a contemporary lighting brand, `rounded-lg` (8px) consistently across CTAs, cards, and inputs would create cohesion. Keep the product card top-rounding as a distinctive element if desired, but align CTA buttons to at least `rounded-md`.

## Top 5 Quick Wins

1. **Remove "Ad Space" placeholders** from all product rails — immediate credibility improvement
2. **Replace `font-mono` with `font-sans`** on: hero body text, category badges, trust signals, nav links (keep `font-mono` for prices only)
3. **Increase product title size** from `text-[11px]` to `text-xs` and switch to `font-sans`
4. **Add a warm accent color** (`#C8A96E` gold) to Tailwind config and apply to "View all" links and CTA hover states
5. **Fix hero featured thumbnails** — link to actual collection pages instead of `#`

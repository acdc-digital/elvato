## Strategic Recommendations

### Near-term (next sprint)

1. **Wire real pricing into product cards** — This is critical for any live traffic. The `getProductPrice` call already returns data; display `cheapestPrice` and add price range display for multi-variant products. Consider showing "From $XX" format for products with many variants.

2. **Build a category mega-menu** — The nav "Collections" link should trigger a dropdown/mega-menu showing all 8 categories with small thumbnails. This is standard for lighting e-commerce with 800+ products and dramatically improves category discovery.

3. **Source "Shop by Room" imagery** — Commission or source 5 lifestyle room photos. This section is a proven conversion pattern for lighting retail and is architecturally complete — it just needs the photography.

4. **Fix "LED-itorial" navigation** — Either create a blog/editorial content page and point the link there, or remove the nav item until content is ready. The duplicate link to `/how-it-works` is confusing.

### Medium-term

5. **Add product reviews/ratings** — Integrate a reviews system (Judge.me, Stamped, or custom) and display star ratings on product cards. For lighting products, social proof is a top-3 conversion factor. The CTA banner claims "5.0 Rating" — this needs substantiation.

6. **Build the promotional card system** — Replace the "Ad Space" placeholder with a real promotional card framework. Options: seasonal promotion, category CTA ("See all 200+ pendants →"), newsletter signup mini-form, or curated editorial pick.

7. **Newsletter / email capture** — Add a mid-page or footer newsletter signup with a lighting-specific hook: "Get design inspiration & exclusive offers." No email capture exists anywhere on the storefront currently.

8. **Design Services landing page** — Multiple CTAs now point to `/design-services`. Verify this page exists and has proper content. If not, build it — it's being prominently promoted.

### Longer-term

9. **Trade/professional program** — Create a dedicated "For Professionals" or "Trade Program" page and add it to the nav. Designers and contractors represent high-value repeat customers in the lighting industry.

10. **Collection page enhancements** — Add hero banners and editorial descriptions to category/collection pages. Currently they're functional but sparse — a lifestyle banner and 2-3 sentence intro would improve both UX and SEO.

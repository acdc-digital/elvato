# Decision Framework

Use this framework for business operating decisions, marketplace expansion, product selection, workflow investment, and operational prioritization.

## Required Analysis

For each initiative, identify:

1. Current bottleneck addressed
2. Highest ROI opportunity
3. Risks
4. Opportunity costs
5. Dependencies
6. Execution complexity

## Scoring

Score each initiative from 1-10:

- Revenue Impact
- Profit Impact
- Strategic Value
- Execution Difficulty
- Time to Realize Value
- Risk

Priority Score:

```text
(Revenue Impact + Profit Impact + Strategic Value) / (Execution Difficulty + Risk)
```

Use the score as a guide, not as an autopilot. A low-effort infrastructure task can score high but should still serve the enterprise-value initiative.

## Mandatory Challenge Questions

- What assumption would make this recommendation wrong?
- What evidence would change the decision?
- What work are we choosing not to do?
- Is this an asset, a one-time task, or a distraction?
- Does this create revenue signal, margin clarity, or operating leverage?

## Stop Rules

Pause or kill an initiative when:

- It no longer addresses the active bottleneck.
- The channel's policy conflicts with the business model.
- Margin is structurally weak.
- Fulfillment reliability cannot support the customer promise.
- The work becomes platform-building without near-term operating evidence.

# Operations Operating System

## Mission

Increase Elvato's enterprise value by turning product, technology, and marketplace assets into measurable revenue, margin, operational capability, and durable competitive advantage.

## Decision Standard

Every major recommendation must answer:

**If we could only complete one initiative this week, which would create the greatest increase in enterprise value?**

## Optimization Targets

Optimize for:

- Revenue growth
- Margin expansion
- Competitive advantage
- Asset creation
- Operational efficiency
- Long-term enterprise value

Do not optimize for activity volume, report count, or tool complexity.

## Operating Cadence

### Weekly

- Review current bottleneck.
- Review active initiatives and score changes.
- Produce or update the weekly KPI report.
- Decide keep, improve, duplicate, pause, or kill for each active channel/product family.
- Pick one highest-leverage initiative for the next week.

### Monthly

- Reassess marketplace/channel strategy.
- Review product-family economics.
- Identify the new constraint.
- Update the 30-day operating plan.

### Quarterly

- Review strategic positioning.
- Review capital allocation.
- Decide whether to deepen current channels, expand channels, or strengthen infrastructure.

## Evidence Hierarchy

1. Actual sales, margin, fulfillment, returns, and customer feedback.
2. Marketplace account-health and policy signals.
3. Listing-level traffic and conversion data.
4. Supplier cost, stock, shipping, and reliability data.
5. Technical readiness.
6. Strategic intuition.

Ideas without measurable operating evidence stay below execution priorities.

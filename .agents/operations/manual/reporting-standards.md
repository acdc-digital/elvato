# Reporting Standards

## Report Location

All dated business operations reports should be stored in:

```text
.agents/operations/reports/
```

Reusable frameworks belong in `manual/`, repeatable procedures in `workflows/`, and reusable formats in `templates/`.

## Report Naming

Use lowercase, date-prefixed names when possible:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md
```

Existing migrated reports may keep their original names.

## Required Sections

Most operating reports should include:

- Executive Summary
- Current Constraint
- Highest-Leverage Initiative
- Reason
- Expected Outcome
- Initiative Ranking
- Risks
- Dependencies
- Recommended Next Action
- Missing Information

## Artifact Discipline

Reports should produce a decision or a workflow. Avoid reports that only summarize context without changing what the business does next.

# Marketplace Realignment Analysis

Date: 2026-06-20
Context: Etsy store banned after listings were flagged as mass-produced / dropship items. Etsy produced at least one sale, validating that buyers respond to the product direction, but Etsy is no longer structurally aligned with Elvato's operating model.

## Executive Summary

Etsy should be treated as a completed learning channel, not a primary go-forward marketplace. The sale on Etsy is important because it validates that the lighting assortment can convert when exposed to marketplace traffic. The ban is equally important because it proves Etsy's handmade / original / vintage positioning is structurally misaligned with Elvato's supplier-powered lighting model.

The best next marketplace for Elvato is **eBay**.

eBay is the most suitable next channel because it is faster to launch, more compatible with reseller / supplier-fulfilled products, less dependent on handmade provenance, and more forgiving for early operating volume than Walmart, Amazon, Wayfair, or Houzz. It is not perfect: eBay still requires accurate item location, reliable fulfillment, no deceptive dropshipping, and strong seller performance. But compared with Etsy, it is much better aligned with Elvato's actual business structure.

Walmart is attractive but should be the second marketplace, not the immediate replacement. Walmart can create more enterprise value later, but it requires stronger operational maturity: UPC/GTIN discipline, reliable shipping SLAs, returns handling, competitive pricing, seller approval, clean product feeds, and performance metrics. Launching Walmart before eBay validates fulfillment and margin would increase compliance and account-risk pressure too early.

Recommended strategy:

1. Stop investing in Etsy as a sales channel.
2. Preserve Etsy learning: winning titles, images, product aesthetics, pricing, and conversion signal.
3. Launch an eBay MVP with 10-20 highly curated lighting listings.
4. Build a marketplace-neutral product feed and KPI workflow in `.agents/operations/reports`.
5. Prepare Walmart only after eBay proves fulfillment, margin, and seller operations.

If we could only complete one initiative this week, the highest-value action is:

**Build and launch an eBay marketplace MVP for 10-15 vetted products, using Etsy's winning lessons but avoiding Etsy's handmade-positioning assumptions.**

## Strategic Reframe

Etsy answered one question: "Can marketplace buyers respond to our lighting assortment?" Yes, at least directionally.

Etsy also answered another question: "Is Etsy the right long-term channel for a supplier-powered lighting operator?" No.

The new operating question is:

**Which marketplace lets Elvato sell curated modern lighting without pretending to be handmade, while giving us enough traffic, manageable compliance risk, and fast feedback?**

That points to eBay first.

## Recommended Marketplace: eBay

### Why eBay Fits Elvato Now

- eBay does not require products to be handmade, vintage, or original in the Etsy sense.
- eBay buyers already shop for lighting fixtures, replacement fixtures, home improvement items, and design-forward decor.
- eBay supports both fixed-price listings and catalog-style selling.
- It is accessible to early-stage sellers without the same approval burden as Walmart.
- It allows the business to learn fulfillment, pricing, customer-service, returns, and product-family demand with lower platform-entry friction.
- It can absorb a curated reseller / supplier-powered assortment if listings are accurate and fulfillment promises are reliable.

### eBay Caveats

eBay is not a free-for-all. It is more aligned than Etsy, but Elvato still needs disciplined compliance:

- Do not imply handmade / in-house manufacturing.
- Use accurate item location and handling time.
- Do not dropship from retail marketplaces or create arbitrage-style customer experience risk.
- Use supplier / wholesale / direct sourcing where Elvato controls the buyer promise.
- Make returns, shipping times, and installation/spec details clear.
- Avoid exaggerated certification, UL/ETL, or safety claims unless verified.

### eBay Launch Thesis

Elvato should position listings as curated modern lighting and home decor, not handmade artisan goods.

The first eBay assortment should use Etsy's learning but filter for operational reliability:

- Strong visual appeal
- Clear product photos
- Manageable variants
- Clear dimensions/specs
- Healthy estimated gross margin
- Reliable supplier stock and shipping
- Low compliance ambiguity

## Walmart Assessment

Walmart is attractive because it has large buyer reach, strong home-goods traffic, and a more mainstream retail model than Etsy. It is better aligned with mass-produced products than Etsy.

However, Walmart should not be the first replacement channel.

### Why Walmart Should Be Second

- Seller approval and onboarding are more involved.
- Product feeds are stricter.
- UPC/GTIN and taxonomy discipline matter more.
- Shipping SLAs and seller performance are less forgiving.
- Walmart buyers expect retail-grade delivery and customer service.
- Account health risk is meaningful if fulfillment is not already reliable.
- Price competition can compress margins.

Walmart becomes attractive after Elvato proves the following on eBay or owned storefront:

- On-time fulfillment can be maintained.
- Return reasons are understood.
- Top product families are known.
- Margin floors are validated.
- Listing data is clean and feed-ready.
- Customer support process is repeatable.

### Walmart Go / No-Go Criteria

Start Walmart only when:

- At least 20-30 products have verified specs, shipping, and margin.
- eBay or owned-store orders show supplier reliability.
- Product identifiers, variants, images, and categories are normalized.
- Return and customer-service process is documented.
- The business can meet Walmart delivery and support expectations without manual panic.

## Other Channel Notes

### Google Merchant Center / Shopping

This should become the owned-channel companion to marketplace selling. It reduces dependence on marketplace policy risk and drives traffic to Elvato's own storefront.

Best use:

- Build a clean product feed from Medusa.
- Start with products proven by eBay or Etsy learning.
- Use Google free listings first, then small paid Shopping tests.

Why not first:

- It requires storefront trust, checkout confidence, feed quality, and ad/traffic management.
- It does not solve marketplace trust as quickly as eBay.

### Wayfair / Perigold

Strategically aligned with lighting and home goods, but likely heavier operationally.

Best use:

- Later-stage supplier / vendor channel once Elvato has reliable product data, packaging, lead times, and margin structure.

Why not first:

- Longer onboarding.
- Higher operational expectations.
- More wholesale/vendor-style economics.

### Houzz

Potentially aligned with home design and remodel buyers, but likely smaller and less immediate than eBay.

Best use:

- Secondary test after eBay listings and product data are stable.

### Amazon

Amazon has large traffic but is not recommended near-term.

Risks:

- Strict dropshipping policy enforcement.
- High account-health pressure.
- Lighting category compliance and product identifier expectations.
- Competitive price pressure.
- Higher support and returns expectations.

Amazon should only be considered after supplier reliability, compliance documentation, product IDs, and fulfillment operations are much stronger.

## Highest-ROI Operating Plan

### Week 1: eBay MVP Setup

Objective: Prepare the first 10-15 eBay listings without creating channel sprawl.

Actions:

- Create an eBay marketplace folder and operating checklist.
- Select 15 products from Etsy winners and top candidate reports.
- Apply a strict go/no-go gate:
  - clear photos
  - clear dimensions
  - verified voltage/wattage/bulb base where applicable
  - no unverified certification claims
  - reliable supplier stock/shipping
  - margin floor cleared
  - manageable variants
- Build an eBay listing template:
  - title
  - category
  - item specifics
  - description
  - condition
  - shipping/handling time
  - return policy
  - images
  - SKU mapping
- Do not launch Walmart yet.

- `.agents/operations/reports/ebay-launch-candidate-queue-YYYY-MM-DD.md`
- First 10-15 listing plans ready for eBay.

Objective: Put products live and begin collecting signal.

  - views/watchers if accessible

  - revenue
  - estimated gross margin
  - questions/messages
  - returns/cancellations

Deliverable:

- `.agents/operations/reports/walmart-readiness-decision-YYYY-MM-DD.md`

## Product Selection Principles

For the first post-Etsy marketplace batch, prioritize products that are easier to fulfill and easier for buyers to understand.

Prefer:

- Table lamps, floor lamps, wall sconces, simple pendants, and simple chandeliers.
- Products with 6-10 clear images.
- Products with 1-6 variants.
- Products with clear dimensions and electrical specs.
- Products in a $50-$250 price band if margin supports it.
- Products that do not depend on unverifiable certification claims.

Avoid initially:

- Very complex chandeliers with many variants.
- Products with unclear installation specs.
- Products with weak supplier data.
- Products with very long shipping promises.
- Products that require safety claims we cannot verify.
- Very high-ticket fixtures before seller trust is established.

## Recommended Positioning

Do not describe Elvato products as handmade, artisan-made, custom-made, or locally manufactured unless factually true.

Use positioning such as:

- curated modern lighting
- contemporary home lighting
- minimalist pendant lights
- glass globe fixtures
- statement lighting for dining rooms, bedrooms, and living spaces
- supplier-sourced / professionally selected lighting designs

The strategic shift is from "artisan marketplace" to "curated lighting retailer."

## Technical Implications

The repo currently has no eBay or Walmart scaffold under `marketplace/Ebay` or `marketplace/Walmart`. The next technical work should be eBay-first:

1. Define a marketplace-neutral listing schema.
2. Create an eBay listing plan generator from Medusa products.
3. Write operations reports to `.agents/operations/reports` and channel artifacts to `reports/ebay` or `marketplace/Ebay`.
4. Add SKU mapping from Medusa/CJ to eBay listing IDs.
5. Build KPI reporting after listings are live.

Do not build Walmart integration until the eBay listing schema proves stable.

## Key Risks

- Repeating Etsy's mistake by choosing a channel whose policies conflict with the operating model.
- Over-listing before fulfillment reliability is proven.
- Margin loss from shipping, marketplace fees, and returns.
- Safety/compliance issues for electrical lighting products.
- Account-health risk from late shipments, cancellations, or inaccurate item location.
- Building heavy automation before the channel proves sales potential.

## Final Recommendation

Move forward with **eBay as the immediate replacement marketplace**.

Use Etsy as learning, not as a continuing channel. The sale showed that the product direction has commercial potential. The ban showed that Etsy's platform thesis conflicts with Elvato's supply model.

The best enterprise-value path is to launch a disciplined eBay MVP, measure sales and fulfillment, then use that proof to decide whether Walmart deserves onboarding investment.

This week, the one initiative that matters most is:

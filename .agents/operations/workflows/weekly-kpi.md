# Weekly KPI Workflow

## Purpose

Create a weekly decision report that shows whether current operations are increasing enterprise value.

## Inputs

- Marketplace listings
- Orders and transactions
- Payments and fees
- Supplier costs
- Shipping costs
- Returns and cancellations
- Customer messages/reviews
- Account-health signals

## Output Location

```text
.agents/operations/reports/YYYY-MM-DD-weekly-kpi.md
```

## Required Decisions

For each channel/product family, mark one:

- Keep
- Improve
- Duplicate
- Pause
- Kill

## Review Questions

- What generated revenue this week?
- What created margin?
- What caused operational drag?
- What evidence changed our priorities?
- What is the one initiative for next week?

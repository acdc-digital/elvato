# Marketplace Launch Workflow

## Purpose

Launch new marketplace channels through a controlled MVP process that validates demand without creating account-health or fulfillment risk.

## Workflow

1. Define channel thesis and policy fit.
2. Confirm seller account readiness.
3. Configure required business policies, credentials, and channel defaults.
4. Generate launch candidate queue.
5. Apply product launch gate.
6. Build listing plans.
7. Publish a small MVP batch.
8. Track performance and account-health signals.
9. Decide whether to deepen, pause, or expand.

## Launch Gate

- Clear hero image and image count.
- Accurate title and item specifics.
- Verified specs and safety/compliance language.
- Supplier stock and fulfillment confidence.
- Margin floor cleared.
- Return policy understood.
- Channel policy fit confirmed.

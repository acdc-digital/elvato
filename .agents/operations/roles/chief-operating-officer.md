# Chief Operating Officer Role

## Mission

Convert Elvato's strategy into focused execution that increases enterprise value.

## Responsibilities

- Identify the active business constraint.
- Rank initiatives by enterprise-value impact.
- Protect focus and prevent duplicated effort.
- Convert analysis into weekly action.
- Ensure operations reports become workflows or decisions.

## Default Questions

- What is the constraint today?
- What is the highest-value action this week?
- What must stop so the main initiative can succeed?
- What operating evidence do we need next?

## Output Standard

Every COO output should end with a clear decision, owner/action, dependency, and next review point.

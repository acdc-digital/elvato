# Business Analyst Role

## Mission

Turn raw marketplace, product, pricing, and operational data into decision-ready analysis.

## Responsibilities

- Build KPI reports.
- Track assumptions and missing data.
- Join product, marketplace, supplier, and margin data.
- Identify anomalies, risks, and performance gaps.

## Core Metrics

- Active listings
- Product-family mix
- Orders and gross sales
- Gross margin dollars and percentage
- Fulfillment time
- Returns, cancellations, and customer issues
- Channel account-health signals

## Output Standard

Analysis should make the next action obvious: keep, improve, duplicate, pause, or kill.

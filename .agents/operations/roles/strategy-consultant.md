# Strategy Consultant Role

## Mission

Evaluate markets, channels, positioning, and competitive strategy with a bias toward measurable enterprise value.

## Responsibilities

- Compare channel options.
- Challenge assumptions.
- Identify opportunity costs.
- Translate market learning into focused operating bets.

## Frameworks

- Theory of Constraints
- McKinsey-style issue trees
- Bain-style operational diagnosis
- Private equity value creation
- Startup growth loops
- Systems thinking

## Output Standard

Recommendations must state why the chosen path beats the alternatives now, not in theory.

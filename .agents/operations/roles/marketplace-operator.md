# Marketplace Operator Role

## Mission

Launch and manage marketplace channels without violating platform policy or overextending operations.

## Responsibilities

- Maintain channel setup checklists.
- Prepare launch candidate queues.
- Enforce listing compliance and margin gates.
- Track account-health and fulfillment risk.
- Maintain channel-specific workflows and reports.

## Active Channels

- eBay: primary post-Etsy MVP channel.
- Etsy: archived learning channel; do not treat as go-forward sales channel.
- Walmart: future candidate after eBay proof.
- Google Merchant Center: owned-store traffic companion after feed readiness.

## Output Standard

Marketplace recommendations must include policy fit, fulfillment promise, margin risk, and account-health risk.

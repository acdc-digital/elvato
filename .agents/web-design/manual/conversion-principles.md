# Conversion & Trust Principles

Durable principles for residential lighting at a $100–$2,000+ price point. Use these to justify or reject design decisions.

## Trust Is the Primary Conversion Lever

At this price point, customers convert when they trust the product and the seller. Prioritize:

- High-quality, zoomable, multi-angle product imagery and in-room context shots.
- Complete specifications: dimensions, materials, finish, bulb type/count, wattage, voltage, dimmable/compatible, weight, mounting, certifications.
- Clear shipping cost, timeline, and packaging assurance for fragile fixtures.
- Plain-language return policy near the buy decision.
- Reviews and credible social proof; supplier/brand credibility.

## Reduce Friction in the Buying Journey

- Make the primary CTA obvious and persistent; one clear next step per screen.
- Keep critical information visible — never behind hover or unnecessary clicks.
- Minimize required fields; support guest checkout.
- Show cost transparency early; no surprise fees at payment.
- Provide fast, reassuring states for loading, empty, error, and success.

## Product Discovery

- Strong category navigation by fixture type (chandeliers, pendants, sconces, flush mounts, ceiling, outdoor).
- Faceted filtering that matches how shoppers decide: style, finish, size, price, room, bulb type.
- Fast, forgiving search (MeiliSearch) with relevant results and zero-result recovery.
- Relevant cross-sell and "complete the look" that aids the decision rather than distracts.

## Average Order Value

- Bundle complementary items (matching fixtures, bulbs, dimmers) without dark patterns.
- Surface relevant upsells at natural decision points, framed as value to the customer.

## Mobile-First Conversion

- Mobile is the primary surface; design and validate it first.
- Thumb-reachable primary actions; sticky add-to-cart where it helps.
- Image-forward layouts that load fast and never shift.

## Performance Is Conversion

- Slow pages lose sales. Protect LCP, CLS, and INP.
- Optimize hero and product imagery; reserve layout space.
- Prefer server rendering and minimal client JS.

## Brand & Marketplace Consistency

- One Elvato brand voice and visual language across storefront, eBay, and future channels.
- Consistent product naming, imagery, and trust language reduce confusion and returns.

## What to Reject

- Trendy design that measurably hurts conversion.
- Excessive animation, unnecessary complexity, hidden information.
- Dark patterns of any kind.
- Anything that increases development complexity without measurable business value.

### Accessing Physical Variant Groups
Each product's `physicalVariantGroups` field contains:
- `groupKey` - Unique identifier for the physical combination
- `physicalOptionValues` - The specific options (e.g., `{"Finish": "Black", "Number of Lights": "3 Lights"}`)
- `variantIds` - Array of variant IDs that share this appearance
- `assignedImageUrl` - Image URL when assigned
